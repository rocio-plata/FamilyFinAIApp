## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined Scandinavian fintech aesthetic: calm, grounded, and subtly luxurious. Designed for household wealth management and AI-guided collective budgeting, the interface replaces financial anxiety with serene clarity, tactile balance, and dignified simplicity.

### Key Pillars
- **Nordic Hygge & Clarity:** Expansive, breathing layouts that banish clutter. Surfaces feel organic, warm, and natural rather than clinical or austere.
- **Understated Financial Authority:** Trust is cultivated through structural precision, balanced typography, and deliberate semantic feedback rather than aggressive alerts.
- **Cognitive Serenity:** High visual hierarchy ensures collective family figures, AI insights, and granular transactions coexist without overwhelming the user.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in deep mineral tones and organic sage, avoiding cold corporate blues in favor of biophilic equilibrium.

### Foundations & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F8FA`):** Warm off-white mineral ground. Provides atmospheric softness behind cards and elevated sheets.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white applied to interactive modules, budget widgets, and insight cards.
- **Border / Structural Stroke (`#E8EBEF`):** Mist gray hairpins used for subtle spatial boundaries without visual noise.

### Core Brand Spectrum
- **Primary Deep Organic Sage (`#1E5E53`):** Primary interactions, authoritative headings, and key active states.
- **Secondary Mineral Slate Teal (`#2D7366`):** Secondary controls, container fills, and chart baseline series.
- **Soft Sage Tint (`#E3ECE9`):** Selected navigation items, subtle pills, and AI prompt backdrops.

### Semantic Spectrum
- **Income / Surplus (`#2F9E74` / Container `#E8F6EE`):** Forest mint green evoking sustainable growth.
- **Expense / Outflow (`#D9615B` / Container `#FDEEEC`):** Muted terracotta/rose coral; provides immediate awareness without anxiety-inducing alarms.
- **Warning / Budget Cap (`#D9822B` / Container `#FDF3E7`):** Soft honey amber for threshold warnings.

### Category Accents
- **Slate Blue (`#4B6B94`)**, **Soft Plum (`#7A5877`)**, **Sage Pine (`#386B5A`)**, **Nordic Sand (`#C4A482`)**, **Mauve (`#9B6A78`)**, and **Terracotta Clay (`#C06352`)**.

## Typography

Inter serves as the primary typographic engine, leveraging its optical balancing and tabular features for numerical clarity.

### Numerical Treatment (CLP Chilean Pesos)
- All currency readouts (e.g., `$1.450.000 CLP`) must enable tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`) to preserve vertical alignment across ledger summaries.
- Currency symbols take a slightly reduced weight or muted tone relative to large transaction amounts to spotlight the magnitude without visual friction.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a responsive 12-column fluid grid system on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column stacked format on mobile devices.

### Form Factor Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1280px`, `2.5rem` canvas margin, `1.5rem` column gutters. Multi-column financial widgets, AI assistant drawer, and ledger splits.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer margins. Side panels fold into overlay sheets or collapsible tabs.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column flow with `1rem` outer margin and `1rem` gutters. Primary quick actions (Add Expense, Scan Receipt) lock to a bottom floating dock.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through quiet layering and soft ambient dispersion rather than high-contrast cast shadows.

### Ambient Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background `#F7F8FA`. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Sheets):** Surface `#FFFFFF` bordered with `1px solid #E8EBEF`. Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 94, 83, 0.04), 0 4px 16px -4px rgba(45, 115, 102, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Active / Hover / Dropdown):** Surface `#FFFFFF`. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 94, 83, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Surface `#FFFFFF`. Shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(20, 48, 43, 0.14)`. Backdrop filter blur: `12px` with a `rgba(30, 50, 47, 0.25)` scrim.

## Shapes

In alignment with modern Material Design 3 guidelines and Nordic organic styling, shapes prioritize smooth, reassuring curves. 

- **Cards, Modules, and Modals:** Use `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`) for standard containers to evoke soft tactile forms.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** Rely on `rounded-md` (8px to 12px) for structured finger targets.
- **Badges, Tags, and Filters:** Retain fully pill-shaped geometries (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata from primary containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E5E53`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `12px`. Subtle hover shift to `#2D7366` with zero sharp border strokes.
- **Secondary / Tonal:** Background `#E3ECE9`, text `#1E5E53`. Hover transition to `#D5E4E0`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with subtle `#1E5E53` text and `4px` padding.

### Cards
- Constructed on `#FFFFFF` ground with `16px` border-radius (`rounded-lg`), subtle `1px solid #E8EBEF` border, and Level 1 ambient shadow. Padding is systematically set to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E8EBEF`, transitioning to a `2px` focus ring in `#1E5E53` with `rgba(30, 94, 83, 0.1)` outer ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved checks and circular radios rendered in `#1E5E53` when active, with smooth spring transitions.

### Chips & Badges
- Category chips utilize low-saturation background tints derived from their category accents (e.g., Soft Plum `#F5EEF4`, Mauve `#F7EFF1`) paired with saturated foreground text, pill-shaped (`9999px`) boundaries, and `0.5rem` vertical padding.

### AI Insight Banners
- Styled with a subtle gradient background flowing from `#E3ECE9` (Soft Sage) to `#FFFFFF`, highlighted with a thin `#2D7366` left indicator stroke and gentle typography for proactive family budget suggestions.
